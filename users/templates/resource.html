Resources

Selected software, lecture notes, and materials used in Quantar Lab. These resources are intended for research and education in quantum algorithms, quantum metrology, and related topics.

Software

tqix

tqix is an open-source Python program for quantum measurement and related topics. It provides tools for quantum states and operators, quantum gates, and tutorials for applications such as quantum state tomography and quantum metrology. It is designed to be adaptable across platforms (quantum optics, ion traps, superconducting circuits, etc.) and useful for classroom simulations and visualizations.
