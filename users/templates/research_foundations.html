Quantum foundations

We study the structure of quantum measurement and correlation itself — nonlocality, entanglement witnesses, and the weak and modular values that describe what postselection and measurement backaction let us learn about a quantum state.

This foundational work feeds directly back into our metrology and algorithms research, since precision and control both run up against the same fundamental limits.
