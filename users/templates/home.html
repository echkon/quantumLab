Quantar Lab

Our lab explores the frontiers of quantum science and technology through the following research directions:

  • Quantum Algorithms and Compilation: Designing efficient algorithms and compilation techniques for near-term and future quantum computers, with applications to optimization, simulation, and entanglement detection.
  • Quantum Metrology and Sensing: Developing time-dependent and networked quantum sensing protocols to achieve precision beyond classical limits, with applications ranging from fundamental physics to biophysics.
  • Entanglement and Quantum Measurement: Investigating strategies for detecting, preserving, and exploiting entanglement, including novel measurement schemes such as nonlocal and variational approaches.
  • Quantum Energy and Batteries: Studying collective quantum systems to design and optimize protocols for energy storage, transfer, and charging in quantum batteries.

By integrating these themes, Quantar Lab aims to advance both fundamental understanding and practical applications of quantum technologies.
