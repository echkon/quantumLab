Featured paper / project

Quantum-enhanced multiphase metrology under squeezing

A practical framework to understand when squeezing helps multi-parameter estimation, and how to approach near-optimal strategies.

  • What: multiphase estimation with nonlinear squeezing dynamics
  • Why it matters: precision improvements beyond classical limits
  • Outcome: conditions and recipes to approach quantum limits
How we work

We combine (i) rigorous theory (QET/QFI/QFIM), (ii) numerics + optimization, and (iii) realistic constraints (noise, finite measurements, hardware limits) to deliver results that remain useful outside ideal settings.
