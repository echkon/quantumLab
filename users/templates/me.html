Email: binho@fris.tohoku.ac.jp

Research Vision

My research vision is to establish a unified framework connecting quantum estimation theory, control, and algorithms with realistic quantum hardware constraints. I aim to develop protocols that remain effective under noise, finite data, and limited control.

  • Quantum metrology & sensing: When and how quantum resources truly enhance multiparameter precision.
  • Quantum algorithms & control: Feedback-based and variational methods with stable optimization.
  • Quantum energy technologies: Collective effects for fast and robust quantum battery charging.
  • Theory to platforms: Translating QFI/QFIM concepts into near-term device protocols.

The long-term goal is to deliver practical quantum advantages in sensing, computation, and energy applications.
