Open Positions

Quantar Lab welcomes motivated students and researchers interested in quantum algorithms, quantum sensing, quantum metrology, and quantum technologies. Below are current and recurring opportunities to join our group.

JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowship

We welcome applications for the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) Postdoctoral Fellowship. This program supports outstanding early-career researchers who wish to conduct independent research in Japan.

Learn more & apply →

FRIS Undergraduate Research Opportunity (URO)

Undergraduate students at Tohoku University are encouraged to participate in research projects through the FRIS URO program. Students gain hands-on experience in cutting-edge research while receiving academic and financial support.

詳細・応募はこちら（日本語） → Learn more (English) →

Research Internships

Undergraduate and graduate students interested in quantum technologies, machine learning, or quantum sensing are welcome to apply for research internships. Projects can be tailored to your background and research interests.

Contact us to discuss opportunities →

Other Opportunities

We occasionally open funded research assistant positions, collaborative research programs, and international exchange opportunities.

If you are interested, please send a CV and a short motivation letter to binho@fris.tohoku.ac.jp.
